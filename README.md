A deadpan museum label from the year 3000 for the most ordinary object of our time: the group chat. In exactly 100 characters, a curator from a millennium from now describes forty-seven humans gathering daily in one place — to ignore one another in perfect unison. The misreading is true and absurd at once: the group chat really is where everyone shows up and nobody listens.
